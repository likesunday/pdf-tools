# Marketing & Link Building Guide

## Product Description (English)

### One-liner
Free online PDF & image tools that process everything in your browser — no uploads, no servers, 100% private.

### Short Description (ProductHunt / directories)
PDF Tools is a free, open-source collection of 22 online tools for working with PDFs and images. Compress, merge, split, rotate PDFs, convert between formats, add watermarks — all processing happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

### Key Features
- 🔒 100% Privacy — files never uploaded to any server
- ⚡ Fast — runs on your device's processing power
- 🌍 Bilingual — English & Chinese
- 💰 Free — no sign-up, no limits
- 📱 Works on mobile & desktop

## Platforms to Submit (Free backlinks)

### High Priority
1. **Product Hunt** — https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new
   - Category: Productivity, Developer Tools
   - Best launch day: Tuesday or Wednesday
   
2. **Hacker News (Show HN)** — https://news.ycombinator.com/submit
   - Title: "Show HN: Free PDF tools that run entirely in your browser (no upload)"
   
3. **Reddit**
   - r/webdev — "I built a free PDF toolkit that processes files locally"
